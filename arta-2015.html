Achaemenid Elamite Administrative Tablets, 3: Fragments from Old Kandahar, Afghanistan

Michael T. Fisher and Matthew W. Stolper

ARTA: Achaemenid Research on Texts and Archaeology 2015.001: 1–26 (January 2015).

This article publishes images and an edition of an Achaemenid Elamite tablet fragment excavated at Old Kandahar in 1977 and rediscovered in the National Museum of Afghanistan in 2013. Its two-way layout and accounting terminology match the journals and accounts of the Persepolis Fortification Archive, implying a lost administrative archive at Kandahar and supporting its identification as the centre of Achaemenid Arachosia.
